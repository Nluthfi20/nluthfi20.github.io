<p>Adhan-Mute is a simple, powerful tool that automatically lowers or mutes your computer’s sound whenever it’s time for the Muslim call to prayer (Adhan). It works quietly in the background with your favorite apps like Spotify, YouTube, Netflix, games, or any other program playing audio. You no longer need to scramble to find your phone, pause your video, or turn down the volume manually. Adhan-Mute does it for you, right on time, every time, wherever you are in the world.</p>

<p>This app is designed for everyone—students, remote workers, gamers, podcast listeners, or anyone with a busy schedule. If you want to stay mindful of prayer times without disrupting your flow, this is your perfect companion.</p>

<h2>💡 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h2>
<p><strong>Q: Will this affect my headphones or external speakers?</strong></p>
<p>A: Yes, it mutes the system master volume, so any output device (headphones, speakers, Bluetooth) is affected. That’s the point—complete silence for the call to prayer.</p>

<p><strong>Q: What happens if I am in a video call (Zoom, Meet)?</strong></p>
<p>A: The system volume will drop, so the other participants may sound quieter. However, your microphone remains unaffected. To avoid interruption, you can enable <strong>“Meeting Mode”</strong> in the settings, which sends you a notification instead of muting.</p>

<p><strong>Q: What if my prayer times are wrong?</strong></p>
<p>A: You can manually adjust with a “time offset” (minutes before/after) in the settings. This is useful if your local mosque calls a few minutes later than calculated time.</p>

<p><strong>Q: Does it work in airplane mode or without internet?</strong></p>
<p>A: Yes. Once the initial prayer times are fetched, it stores them locally for months ahead. No internet is needed during daily use.</p>

<p><strong>Q: Is it safe? Does it contain viruses?</strong></p>
<p>A: Absolutely safe. The app is open-source,meaning its code is public for anyone to inspect. It just controls volume an not touch any personal files.</p>
